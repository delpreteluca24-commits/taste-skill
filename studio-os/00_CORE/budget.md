## Pre-spend checklist (required)
1. Experiment ID identified
2. Expected learning written down
3. Available credits checked
4. Expected credit use estimated (from **current** pricing, re-checked)
5. Remaining budget after spending ≥ reserve
6. Founder approval needed? (always, at LEVEL ≤3)
→ If any item is unclear: **STOP. Ask the founder.**

## CFO verdicts
`APPROVED` · `CAUTION` · `BLOCKED` — the CFO can block, but can't override the founder.

## Never
Automatic subscriptions · auto-refill · spending all credits · generation loops · paid products without approval · assuming ROI.
