# Mission
Build an original, calm, **language-first preschool IP (ages 0–5)** (codename PROJECT M) into a recognizable, coherent and scalable children's brand.

- Long-term financial target: **€5,000+/month revenue** (a business target, NOT a prediction or guarantee). YouTube ads alone are unlikely to get there; books, sound books, printables, music and licensing are part of the plan from day one.
- Validation capital: **€300 max**. Goal: the most learning and the most reusable assets for the least unnecessary spending.

## Positioning (HYPOTHESIS, approved to test, D-004)
Calm pacing + a pause-and-repeat language mechanic + no dialogue (so it works in any language) + a collectible cast. Evidence and market gap: offline concept bible §1 (to be committed after FD-002).

## IP value chain
CHARACTER → WORLD → STORIES → VISUAL ASSETS → VIDEO → AUDIO → AUDIENCE → BOOKS → PRINTABLES → MUSIC → PRODUCTS → LICENSING → MERCHANDISING

For every asset, ask: *can this asset create value in another format?*
