## FINDINGS

### Lilla
- **FACT** — In Italian, "lilla" is an ordinary word for a colour (between pink and violet, the colour of lilac flowers). Source: [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/vocabolario/lilla/), [Wikipedia IT – Lilla (colore)](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilla_(colore)).
- **FACT** — In Swedish, "lilla" means "little" (the definite or plural form of *liten*). It is used in many titles, for example the children's book series *Lilla Anna och Långa farbrorn* (Inger & Lasse Sandberg, from 1964), and *Lilla spöket Laban*. Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilla_Anna_och_L%C3%A5nga_farbrorn), [Wikipedia – Inger and Lasse Sandberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inger_and_Lasse_Sandberg).
- **FACT** — Lilla is a common Hungarian first name (a diminutive of Lívia/Lídia). Source: [Behind the Name](https://www.behindthename.com/name/lilla).
- **FACT** — There are existing YouTube channels with the name: "Lilla TV", "Lilla Productions", "Lilla Plays", "Lilla katten". Their size and content were not checked. Source: web search (YouTube results).
- **FACT** — Spotify has several artists named Lilla: "LILLA", "Lillà", "Lilla Lovis" (~13.7K monthly listeners, adult content), "Lilla Anna", "Lilla Bandet" and others. Source: [Spotify LILLA](https://open.spotify.com/artist/1hnWl9T4patzvl6csly12D), [Spotify Lillà](https://open.spotify.com/artist/6NU3vPwjlPTMi5g8SkV9WU), [Lilla Lovis – Wikipedia SV](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilla_Lovis).
- **FACT** — `lilla.com` is used by the L'illa Diagonal shopping centre in Barcelona ([Brandfetch](https://brandfetch.com/lilla.com)). US trademarks exist that contain the word: "LILLA P" (clothing), "LILLA CAVALLO", "LILLA MEJ" (infant clothing, expired), "LILLA DU" (children's apparel). Source: [Justia – LILLA P](https://trademark.justia.com/781/13/lilla-p-78113991.html), [Justia – LILLA MEJ](https://trademark.justia.com/723/34/lilla-mej-72334995.html), [Justia – Elizabeth Tornell (LILLA DU)](https://trademarks.justia.com/owners/elizabeth-tornell-3596462).
- **HYPOTHESIS** — For a *lilac-coloured* character sold in Italy, "LILLA" alone may be judged **descriptive or weakly distinctive** for goods in classes 16/25/28. That makes it harder to register and to enforce. A compound mark (word + distinctive element, or a figurative mark) would probably be stronger.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — Search discoverability for the bare word "Lilla" will be poor: colour results in IT, "little" in SV, and a first name in HU.

### Interpretation
- **HYPOTHESIS** — "Lilla" + **unicorn** (direction A) lands close to *Lily the Unicorn* in English markets (similar sound, same species, same audience) and to *Lillifee + little unicorn* in German markets. This raises the risk of brand confusion and of losing search results for **direction A only**. Directions B (creature) and C (lantern) don't have this problem.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — A pink/lilac, fairy/princess visual style would push Lilla towards Lillifee's territory. The Creative agent should steer G1-S2 studies away from those codes.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — The "Lil-/Lili-" prefix is crowded in kids' YouTube. A distinctive compound brand (Recommendation 3) becomes more important.
